🔬 Operational Healthcare Strategy

1. Protect Same-Day Slots:
Same-day bookings have the highest attendance rate in outpatient medicine (over 95%). Clinics should preserve ~15-20% capacity for same-day walk-in or acute slots.

2. Targeted Multi-Touch Reminders:
Rather than sending generic reminders, patients with $\text{LeadDays} \ge 7$ and prior no-show flags receive targeted confirmation calls 48 hours prior with self-service 1-click reschedule links.

3. Overbooking & Waitlist Safeguards:
High-risk slots are flagged for controlled overbooking or rapid standby queues to protect physician billable hours without adding patient wait time.
